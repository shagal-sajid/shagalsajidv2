Part 2: LinkedIn "Highly Reachable" Strategy

LinkedIn relies heavily on its internal Recruiter Search algorithm. To make your profile highly discoverable, we must optimize it systematically.

The SEO Headline

Search Algorithm Optimization

Your headline is the #1 factor in LinkedIn's search algorithm. Never use a generic title like just "Software Engineer".

Software Engineer (Laravel/PHP) | Machine Learning & Data Pipeline Architect | Python | Data Science (Kaggle)

Why? This headline hits all the major technical keywords tech recruiters actively type into the Recruiter Search bar.

🔹 Core Stack: Laravel, PHP, MySQL, Python, scikit-learn, Scrapy.
🔹 Explore my full portfolio, projects, and technical writing at: shagalsajid.com"

The Content Flywheel (Website ↔ LinkedIn)

To establish authority, you need to drive traffic back and forth between your platforms:

  • Cross-Pollination: Whenever you add a new project or blog post to the website, write a short, engaging text post on LinkedIn summarizing the problem you solved.
  • Action Verbs in Experience: Update your job histories using strong verbs (e.g., Architected, Engineered, Optimized) and quantify impact (e.g., "processed 10k+ records").
  • Call to Action (CTA): Always end LinkedIn posts with a link back to your site: "Read the full technical breakdown on my portfolio: shagalsajid.com"
  • Featured Section: Add your portfolio URL, Kaggle profile, and GitHub to the 'Featured' carousel right below your LinkedIn About section.
